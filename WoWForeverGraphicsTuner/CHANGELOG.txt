WoW Forever Graphics Tuner
===========================

1.1.0
- Adds the available raid-specific graphics controls to the curated panel.
- Saves every readable graphics-related CVar discovered in the running client, not just sliders in the panel.
- Exposes a broader runtime scan and guarded manual CVar editor for settings not represented by sliders.
- Blocks the removed volumeFog toggle and legacy TBC-only shadow CVars from manual changes.
- Checks the auto-apply toggle again before delayed profile restore.

1.0.0
- First separate addon for WoW: Forever, Interface 16001.
- Uses the modern C_CVar and C_Console APIs, not the old TBC 2.4.3 CVar assumptions.
- Discovers settings in the active client and hides missing CVars.
- Saves and restores an account-local profile; no Pocotli preset is bundled.
- Includes runtime scan and a guarded manual CVar editor.
- Does not expose the removed volumeFog off-switch and does not allow giQuality=0.
